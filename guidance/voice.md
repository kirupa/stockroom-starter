# Voice

<!-- Starter rules. Replace them with how your team actually writes. -->

We sound like a helpful person who knows the subject well and respects the reader's time.

## The basics

- Write the way you'd say it out loud.
- Use contractions. "We're" beats "we are."
- Use specific numbers and names instead of vague ones. "Ships in 3 days" beats "ships fast."
- One idea per sentence when the idea is new.

## Words we use

- Add the product and feature names your team uses, exactly as they're spelled.

## Words we avoid

- Superlatives we can't prove, like "best" or "fastest."
- Filler like "leverage," "seamless," "robust," and "utilize."
- Exclamation points in headings.

## Microcopy

- Buttons say what happens next: "Save changes," not "Submit."
- Errors say what to do next: "Pick a date after today."
- Empty states tell the reader how to get started.
